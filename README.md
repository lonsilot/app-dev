# MyFavoriteSeries
*All of us are dead*

**All of Us Are Dead** is a high-octane South Korean zombie horror series on Netflix that centers on a sudden, catastrophic virus outbreak at Hyosan High School. Triggered by a desperate science teacher's failed experiment to help his bullied son, the highly contagious virus spreads rapidly through the student body, transforming the campus into a blood-soaked battleground within hours. Cut off from the outside world without phones, food, or government rescue, a resilient group of teenage classmates must form uneasy alliances and use whatever school supplies they can find to fight off their mutated peers. As the outbreak spills over into the surrounding city, the narrative explores intense themes of survival, adolescent loyalty, and systemic corruption, distinguishing itself from standard zombie thrillers by introducing "half-zombies"—infected individuals who retain their human consciousness while gaining superhuman strength and predatory instincts.
